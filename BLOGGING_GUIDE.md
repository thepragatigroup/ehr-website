# Express HR Solutions — Blogging Guide

A quick reference for the content team. Covers CMS access, writing posts, and generating images that match the site's visual style.

---

## 1. Accessing the CMS

1. Go to **[www.expresshrsolutions.com/admin/](https://www.expresshrsolutions.com/admin/)**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**
3. Authorise the app — you'll land on the CMS dashboard

> You need a GitHub account with Write access to the repository. Contact Rahul if you don't have access yet.

---

## 2. Writing a Blog Post

From the dashboard, click **Blog Posts → New Blog Post**.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Keep under 60 characters. Be specific — "5 Labour Law Changes Affecting Contract Staffing in 2025" beats "Labour Law Update" |
| **Publish Date** | Set to your intended publish date |
| **Category** | Choose one: Industry Insights, Compliance, Case Study, Company News |
| **Excerpt** | 1–2 sentences, max 160 characters. This shows in blog cards and Google search results — make it count |
| **Featured Image** | Upload via Cloudinary (see Section 3). Required. |
| **Featured on Homepage** | Toggle on only if this is a top post. Max 3 featured at a time — the 3 most recent featured posts show in the homepage carousel |
| **Body** | Write in Markdown. Use `##` for section headings, `**bold**` for emphasis |

When done, click **Publish**. The site rebuilds and goes live in about 60 seconds.

## 3. Generating the Featured Image

Every post needs a **16:9 image** (ideally 1200×675px or larger). Use ChatGPT (GPT-4o) or DALL·E 3 with the prompt template below.

### Prompt Template

Copy this exactly and fill in the `[TOPIC]` line only:

```
Cinematic photorealistic image of [TOPIC].

Style: dramatic warm amber and orange lighting, dark moody backgrounds,
professional industrial setting. Shot like a high-end editorial photograph —
deep shadows, golden practicals, strong depth of field with a sharp subject
and soft background bokeh.

People: South Asian workers or supervisors, authentic and confident.
If wearing hi-vis, use orange or yellow vests. No logos on clothing.

Mood: aspirational, powerful, grounded. Not sterile or stock-photo.

Aspect ratio: 16:9. Photorealistic. No text in the image.
```

### Topic Examples by Category

**Industry Insights**
> a warehouse supervisor reviewing inventory data on a tablet, large logistics facility in the background, forklifts moving in the distance

**Compliance**
> a compliance officer at a desk reviewing documents, warm office light, professional setting, paperwork and a laptop visible

**Case Study**
> a team of workers in orange hi-vis vests walking through a large industrial facility, golden backlight at the end of the corridor, sense of scale and purpose

**Company News**
> a diverse group of HR and operations professionals in a meeting room, collaborative atmosphere, urban Mumbai office, warm afternoon light

**Last Mile Delivery**
> a delivery rider on a motorbike navigating a busy Indian city street, warm golden hour light, urban backdrop, sense of speed and purpose

**3PL / Warehousing**
> wide-angle shot inside a vast modern warehouse, rows of tall shelving stacked with goods, a lone supervisor in an orange vest overseeing operations, dramatic overhead lighting

### Tips
- If the face doesn't look South Asian, add: *"Indian features, South Asian appearance"* to the people line
- If the image looks too clean or stock-photo-ish, add: *"gritty, documentary, real-world feel"*
- Regenerate until you have something that feels cinematic — the first result isn't always the best
- Download at the highest resolution available before uploading

---

## 4. Uploading the Image in the CMS

1. In the **Featured Image** field, click the image icon
2. Click **Upload** → select your generated image
3. Cloudinary stores it automatically — no manual file management needed

---

## 5. SEO Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Title is specific and under 60 characters
- [ ] Excerpt is filled in (max 160 characters)
- [ ] Featured image is uploaded
- [ ] Category is selected
- [ ] Body has at least 2–3 `##` section headings
- [ ] Post is at least 400 words

---

## 6. Style Guidelines

- **Voice**: Direct, confident, expert. We're practitioners, not academics.
- **Avoid**: Jargon overload, vague intros ("In today's fast-paced world…"), bullet-point-only posts
- **Lead with the insight**: Tell the reader the main point in the first paragraph, then explain it
- **Use real numbers where possible**: "15,000 workers deployed" beats "thousands of workers"
