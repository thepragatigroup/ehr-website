In March 2025, Reliance's logistics team contacted us on a Thursday afternoon with an urgent requirement: 800 trained warehouse workers needed to be operational at a new facility in Navi Mumbai by Sunday morning. This is the story of how we did it.

## The Challenge

The facility had just received its occupancy certificate after a delayed construction timeline. Reliance had planned for a phased ramp-up over three weeks. The accelerated timeline meant we needed to compress everything — sourcing, verification, induction, and deployment — into 72 hours.

## Day 1: Mobilisation (Thursday, 4 PM – Midnight)

Our operations team immediately activated our existing candidate pool across Mumbai, Thane, and Navi Mumbai. We had approximately 1,200 pre-verified candidates in our database who matched the profile: warehouse pickers and packers with at least 6 months of relevant experience.

By midnight, we had confirmed availability from 920 candidates — giving us a 15% buffer above the requirement.

## Day 2: Verification and Documentation (Friday)

All 920 candidates were processed through:

- **Identity verification** — Aadhaar + one photo ID
- **PF UAN mapping** — ensuring existing UAN accounts were linked correctly
- **Medical fitness check** — basic physical at 3 mobile camps across the catchment area
- **Safety induction** — 2-hour briefing on warehouse protocols, equipment, emergency procedures

By Friday evening, 847 candidates had cleared all checks. We had our 800, with 47 reserves.

## Day 3: Deployment (Saturday – Sunday)

Shift 1 (800 workers) arrived at the facility at 6 AM on Sunday via 22 hired buses from 6 assembly points across the region. All workers were in uniform, had their ID badges, and had completed induction.

The facility was operational by 8 AM — two hours ahead of Reliance's internal deadline.

## What Made It Possible

Three things enabled this deployment:

1. **Pre-verified candidate database** — We don't start sourcing when a requirement comes in. Our talent pipeline is continuously maintained.
2. **Compliance infrastructure** — Because all documentation processes are standardised, we could run them in parallel rather than sequentially.
3. **Geographic spread** — Assembly points in multiple locations meant no single bottleneck in candidate transport.

This is what 15 years of operations in Mumbai looks like in practice.
