This is one of the most critical and high-stakes questions a leadership team will face this quarter. Activating your backup staffing plan too early burns precious capital. Activating it too late results in catastrophic service failures, lost revenue, and lasting brand damage.

Relying on a "gut feeling" is not a strategy. The answer must be rooted in a pre-defined, data-driven protocol that removes emotion and hesitation from the decision-making process.

### **A: You trigger the plan the moment any *one* of the following data thresholds is breached.**

The decision should be automatic, not debated in a lengthy meeting while your operations are failing. Here is a clear, three-tier framework to guide that instant, data-driven decision.

#### **The Three-Tier Trigger Framework**

1. Volume & Velocity Metrics (Leading Indicator)

This trigger is your early warning system. It tells you that demand is fundamentally outstripping your operational plan before the downstream effects are felt.

- **The Trigger:** IF Actual Order Volume > 125% of Forecast for > 4 consecutive hours.

2. Internal SLA Breaches (Lagging Indicator)

This trigger signals that your current team and processes are already at their breaking point. Key internal functions are failing, and a backlog is actively building.

- **The Trigger:** IF critical internal metrics like 'Time-to-Pick' or 'Dock-to-Stock' times > 150% of the standard for > 2 consecutive hours.

3. Primary Vendor Reliability Metrics (Risk Indicator)

This trigger focuses on your supply chain of people. It indicates that your primary staffing partner is unable to meet their commitments, forcing you to activate your backup plan to mitigate risk.

- **The Trigger:** IF your primary staffing partner's 'Fill Rate' for scheduled surge staff drops below 90% OR their 'Time-to-Fill' exceeds the agreed SLA.

#### **The Governance: Activating the 'War Room'**

A trigger is useless without a clear and immediate action plan. The moment any of the above thresholds are met, the following non-negotiable protocol should commence.

Step 1: Automated Alert & Assembly (0-30 Minutes)

An automated alert (SMS/email) is sent to a pre-defined "War Room" list: the Head of Ops, Head of HR, Head of Logistics, and your designated lead from your contingency staffing partner. This group assembles for a mandatory 15-minute virtual confirmation call. The purpose is not to debate the decision, but to confirm its activation.

Step 2: Role Clarity & Deployment (30-60 Minutes)

The War Room's sole focus is execution.

- **Ops Lead:** Confirms the exact headcount, skill requirements, and deployment locations.
- **HR Lead:** Ensures on-site leaders are ready for rapid onboarding and compliance checks.
- **Contingency Partner Lead:** Acknowledges receipt of the request, activates their talent pool, and provides a firm ETA for the first wave of personnel.

Step 3: Continuous Monitoring

The War Room remains active, with brief check-in calls every four hours, until all operational KPIs have returned to their standard, acceptable range.
